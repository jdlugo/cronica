Compare offer-to-tap and tap-to-granted conversion, rewarded revenue per puzzle starter, puzzle solve/run-completion rates, and D1/D7 retention. Compare equal observation windows and break down by language/country where sample sizes permit. Report sample sizes and allow retention cohorts to mature.
